# Contoso Travel & Expense (T&E) Policy Handbook

Version 4.2 | Owner: Finance Policy Office | Applies to all Contoso employees and contractors

## 1. Principles

1.1 Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own.
1.2 Business travel must be necessary, approved where this handbook requires it, and booked through ContosoGo, the corporate booking tool.
1.3 Only the Finance Policy Office can grant exceptions to this handbook (see Section 9). Managers, directors and executives cannot waive a rule on their own, and nobody can approve their own expenses.

## 2. Air travel

2.1 Book economy class for all flights shorter than 6 hours.
2.2 Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.
2.3 Business class is allowed only for flights of 8 hours or longer, and only with written VP approval obtained before booking. First class is never reimbursed.
2.4 Book at least 14 days before departure. Bookings made less than 7 days before departure require manager approval in ContosoGo.
2.5 Seat upgrades, extra-legroom seats and airline lounge access are not reimbursable. The first checked bag is reimbursable.
2.6 Change fees are reimbursable only when the business required the change and the manager approved it.

## 3. Lodging

3.1 Nightly hotel caps (room plus tax):
- Tier 1 cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Zurich): $300 per night.
- Tier 2 cities (Boston, Chicago, Seattle, Los Angeles, Austin, Paris, Berlin, Singapore, Sydney): $220 per night.
- Tier 3 (every other location): $160 per night.
3.2 Book hotels through ContosoGo to get the negotiated corporate rates.
3.3 Suites, room upgrades, minibar, in-room movies, spa and gym fees are not reimbursable.
3.4 Stays longer than 14 consecutive nights must use an extended-stay apartment booked through ContosoGo and approved by the Finance Policy Office.
3.5 Laundry is reimbursable up to $25 per week of stay for trips of 5 or more nights.

## 4. Meals and per diem

4.1 Per diem: $75 per day for domestic trips (breakfast $15, lunch $20, dinner $40). International per diem is $95 per day in Tier 1 cities and $80 per day everywhere else.
4.2 Claim either the per diem or actual costs up to the per diem amounts, never both. No receipts are needed for the per diem; actual costs need itemized receipts.
4.3 When a meal is provided (conference, hotel breakfast, client host), deduct that meal's portion from the per diem.
4.4 Alcohol is never reimbursable, even for client dinners or when it appears on a larger bill.
4.5 Client entertainment: up to $100 per person per event, with pre-approval from a director or above and an itemized receipt that lists all attendees. Alcohol is excluded.

## 5. Ground transportation

5.1 Use public transport, airport shuttles, or standard taxi / rideshare. Premium tiers such as black-car services are not reimbursable.
5.2 Rental cars are allowed only when public transport is impractical, require manager approval, and must be compact or midsize class. Decline the rental company's insurance; the corporate policy already covers it.
5.3 Personal vehicle use for business is reimbursed at $0.70 per mile. Commuting is not reimbursable.
5.4 Airport parking is reimbursable up to $20 per day with a receipt. Tolls are reimbursable with a receipt.

## 6. Receipts and expense reports

6.1 An itemized receipt is required for every expense of $25 or more (per diem meals excepted).
6.2 For a lost receipt, a signed Missing Receipt Affidavit (form TE-14) is required. Each employee may use it at most 2 times per calendar quarter.
6.3 Submit the expense report within 30 days after the trip ends. Reports submitted 31 to 90 days after the trip need a written explanation from the employee's manager. Reports submitted more than 90 days after the trip are not reimbursed.

## 7. Approvals

7.1 Expense reports under $2,500 are approved by the employee's manager. Reports from $2,500 to $9,999.99 are approved by a director. Reports of $10,000 or more are approved by a VP.
7.2 Pre-trip approval (form TR-1) is required for all international travel and for any trip estimated to cost more than $3,000.
7.3 Nobody may approve their own expenses or the expenses of someone more senior.

## 8. Corporate card and non-reimbursable items

8.1 Book airfare and lodging with the corporate card. Use a personal card only where the corporate card is not accepted.
8.2 Personal charges accidentally made on the corporate card must be repaid within 30 days.
8.3 Never reimbursable: alcohol, traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment, travel insurance, companion or family travel, pet fees, souvenirs, and extra costs caused by personal side trips.
8.4 Customer gifts: up to $50 per person per year, with manager pre-approval.

## 9. Exceptions

9.1 Only the Finance Policy Office (finance-policy@contoso.example) can grant exceptions to this handbook.
9.2 Request an exception with form TE-22 before incurring the expense. Retroactive exceptions are not granted, except for documented emergencies reported within 5 business days.
9.3 The T&E Policy Assistant cannot approve expenses, bookings or exceptions. It only explains this handbook.

## 10. Out of scope

This handbook does not cover the tax treatment of expenses, visa or immigration requirements, legal advice, or HR matters such as conduct, performance or payroll. Contact Tax (tax@contoso.example), Global Mobility (mobility@contoso.example), Legal (legal@contoso.example) or HR (hr@contoso.example) respectively.
